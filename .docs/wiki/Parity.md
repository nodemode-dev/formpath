# Parity

formpath measures itself against Adobe Illustrator row by row, the way formvis measures against Photoshop. `docs/PARITY.md` holds the table: 76 scored rows in nine categories, each graded Full (1), Partial (0.5) or None (0) only when its data model, command, interface and render or export path exist in code, plus 12 additions from plan v0.5.0 and four platform rows, shown and not scored.

At v0.45.0 the score is 65.5 of 76 (86.2 percent): 55 Full, 21 Partial, 0 None, from a seventh independent audit that drove every row through the interface; the additions grade 11 of 12. It is the baseline from here: the Partial rows are interface defects, each named in docs/PARITY.md. Before it, at v0.44.0 the self-graded score was 75.5 of 76 (99.3 percent), with only `.ai` Partial, after editable PSD export, linked placement through file handles and a live 3D rotation widget. At v0.43.0 the self-graded score was 74 of 76 (97.4 percent), after Smart Guides path and intersection snapping and fixes to Scissors, Knife and Eraser; the seventh audit grades it again. At v0.42.0 the score was 73 of 76 (96.1 percent): 70 Full, 6 Partial, 0 None, from a sixth independent audit of the code at v0.41.0; the additions grade 11.5 of 12. It confirmed Layers and Distort and Transform as Full and graded Placed images, `.ai`, Raster export, 3D, Smart Guides and Scissors and Knife Partial. Before it, at v0.40.0 the self-graded score was 75.5 of 76 (99.3 percent): 75 Full, 1 Partial (`.ai`), 0 None, after building the four rows the fifth audit found short (Layers, Distort and Transform, Raster export, Placed images); the additions grade 12 of 12. A sixth independent audit grades it again. Independent audits graded the code without this page at v0.16.1 (52 of 76), v0.23.0 (72.5) and v0.30.0 (71, after which the three rows it found short were built).
