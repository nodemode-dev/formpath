# illustration-workflows

## Purpose

Give the director a proven order of tool calls for a kind of deliverable, so the build starts from a structure rather than improvisation. Hands back the recipe with its numbers filled in for the brief.

## Preconditions

The brief is restated and the artboard size is known (`harness-director`).

## Procedure

1. Pick the recipe in `references/recipes.md` that matches the brief; mix two when the brief does.
2. Fill in its numbers from the artboard size: margins at 6 to 8 percent of the shorter side, a type scale from the title down by a ratio of about 1.5.
3. Hand the steps to the director, loading the specialist named at each step for its details.

## Gate

Every step of the chosen recipe has concrete numbers and a tool name before the first draw call.

## Hands back

The ordered plan: tool, parameters, the specialist that owns it.

## Owned resources

- `references/recipes.md` the recipes, each a numbered list of tool calls with the checks between them.
