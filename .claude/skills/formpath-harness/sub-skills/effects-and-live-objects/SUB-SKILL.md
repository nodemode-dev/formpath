# effects-and-live-objects

## Purpose

Add depth and repetition that stay editable until the person decides to expand them. Hands back which objects carry live effects or are live objects.

## Preconditions

The base shapes exist and are painted.

## Procedure

1. **Discover parameters.** `apply_effect` with list true returns every effect id with its parameters and defaults; pass only the parameters to change in `params`. Pass `item` to put the effect on one fill or stroke instead of the whole object (any effect but 3D).
2. **Depth.** stylize.drop_shadow (opacity 30 to 50, blur 6 to 12, offsets 4 to 8 points) and stylize.outer_glow; stylize.inner_glow for embossed looks; blur.gaussian for soft backgrounds.
3. **Character.** distort.roughen for hand-made edges, distort.zig_zag for waves, distort.pucker_bloat for flowers, stylize.round_corners to soften polygons, warp.arc, warp.flag and the other warps for banners and type.
   - **Painterly and print looks** come from the Effect Gallery's raster effects, applied with the same `apply_effect`: gallery.watercolor, gallery.dry_brush or gallery.colored_pencil for illustration, gallery.halftone_pattern, gallery.color_halftone or gallery.mezzotint for print texture, gallery.stained_glass, gallery.crystallize or gallery.mosaic_tiles for cells, gallery.charcoal, gallery.stamp or gallery.photocopy for two-tone (set foreground and background colours), gallery.texturizer for canvas or brick, blur.radial spin or zoom for motion. Sizes are in points and look the same at any zoom. They export to PDF and SVG as images at the document's raster resolution, so put them on the parts that should be pixels and keep type and logos clean. rasterize.raster pixelates on purpose (resolution 10 to 30 ppi).
   - **3D** with `three_d`: kind extrude (depth 20 to 80 points, bevel rounded with bevel_height 2 to 6 for soft edges) for logos, type and buttons; revolve for bottles, vases and cups (draw only the right half of the profile, open, starting and ending on the left edge, then revolve from left); inflate for puffy icons and stickers; plane to lay flat art in perspective. Start from position off_axis_front or isometric_left, then adjust rx, ry, rz in small steps and check with `get_image`. Use material metal for chrome, matte for clay, and shading_color a darker tone of the fill rather than black for friendlier colour. Type and groups turn as one object. To put a label, logo or pattern on a box or a tilted card, make the art a symbol first (`symbol` action create with a name), then pass `map_symbol` with that name on an extrude or plane; size it with map_scale (100 is the symbol's own size) and nudge it with map_x, map_y in points. Leave shade_art on so the label darkens with the face; turn it off for a printed-sticker look. For grounding, `shadow` true with shadow_position below and softness 6 to 12 reads as a cast shadow on the floor; behind suits floating badges. Inflate tilts with rx, ry, rz like the others, but takes no Map Art or shadow. To wrap a picture or a symbol around every surface (wood grain, fabric, a photo on a box), pass `texture` (a symbol name, an embedded image key, or the id of an image placed with `place_image`); texture_scale 100 fits the object's longer side, texture_rotation and texture_offset_x, texture_offset_y place it, and vector mode shows its average colour. `roughness` and `metallic` (0 to 1, ray_trace on) widen or sharpen the highlight and add metal reflection to any material; `reset_surface` returns to the material's own look.
4. **Envelopes** reshape groups and type: `envelope` warp with a style and bend, mesh with rows and columns, or top_object to pour the art into the top shape. To hand-shape a mesh, call info for the point indices and handle ends, then move_point (x, y or dx, dy; handle right, left, down or up to bend an edge) and add_mesh_line where the art needs another row and column; a warp turns into a mesh the first time a point moves, or reset_mesh converts it on purpose. Type stays editable inside: edit_contents, then set_text on the type's id, then edit_envelope. options fidelity 80 to 100 follows tight meshes closely; distort_appearance bends strokes with the art. For a quick perspective on any object, `transform` with perspective (points to pull the top corners in) or corners (four [x, y]) does Free Distort and Perspective Distort; type and images become envelope meshes.
5. **Blends** make in-between steps: select two shapes, `make_blend` with steps, or spacing smooth for colour gradients between shapes.
6. **Repeats**: `repeat` radial (instances, radius) for flowers, dials and mandalas; grid (rows, columns, spacing) for patterns; mirror for symmetry.
7. **Symbols**: `symbol` create from art, then place instances; editing the symbol updates every instance. Group the instances into a set and vary it with `symbolism`: stain toward a colour, size or spin with method random for a natural scatter, screen for depth, scrunch to cluster. Give x, y and radius to change only one area; `repeat` strengthens a step.
8. **Brushes**: `apply_brush` with list true to see the brushes; calligraphic for pen-like strokes, art brushes to stretch art along a path, bristle brushes for painterly strokes. `brush_library` list shows arrows, borders, decorative_scatter, artistic_ink and bristle; `apply_brush` with `library` and `brush` applies one directly. `new_brush` makes a bristle brush (shape, size, density, paint_opacity), an art brush with `scale_mode` guides so ends keep their shape, or a pattern brush from art plus outer, inner, start and end tiles named by pattern swatches (`make_pattern` first).
9. **Patterns**: `make_pattern` to turn a motif into a fill; `edit_pattern` to change its tile (grid, brick, hex), spacing, brick offset or tile size afterwards, which updates every object filled with it.
   - **Generated with the person's model**: `generate_patterns` with a prompt, `colors` (up to 12) or a `preset`, `density` 1 to 5 and `variations` saves each tile as a pattern swatch (`fill_selection` fills the ids with the first). `generative_shape_fill` fills a closed shape with editable art from a prompt (`shape_strength` 0 to 100, `detail`); the result is a clipping group with the shape last. `text_to_vector`, `generative_shape_fill` and `generate_patterns` share `detail` (1 to 10, or low, medium, high; it sets limits on element count, gradients, strokes and compound shapes), `style_preset` (flat, line_art, isometric, hand_drawn, gradient, retro, minimal), `style_reference` (the id of artwork or a placed image whose palette, strokes, corners and structure are read), `effects` (halftone, grain, glow, shadow) and `color_tone` or `palette` (up to 8 colours; every colour is remapped into it after generation). The result lists what was dropped or repaired in the model's SVG. `generate_patterns` also takes `tile_size`, `seamless`, `layout`, `h_spacing` and `v_spacing`. All use the language model the person set for the Assistant; when none is set they fail with that message, so tell the person to set one in Preferences > Plugins rather than retrying.
10. **Fill Regions** colours line art: draw the lines with `draw_path`, `live_paint` make, then fill at a point inside each area; the answer lists a point inside every face. If an area will not fill, its lines leave a gap: set `gap` (3 to 12 points). Moving the lines later keeps the colours.
11. **Intertwine** weaves overlapping objects over and under each other (chain links, a ribbon through a ring): `intertwine` make with the ids, then flip at a point inside each overlap that should change (with three or more objects each flip brings the next one down to the top), or area with a loop of points around part of an overlap. Release gives back the objects; expand makes plain clipped groups.
12. **Gradient mesh** for soft, painterly colour inside a shape: `gradient_mesh` make (rows and columns 3 to 5), list to see the points, then point with a colour (white near the light, a darker tone at the edge); add_line where a highlight needs its own point.
13. **Image trace**: `place_image` then `image_trace` with a preset (black_and_white_logo, 6_colors, silhouettes, line_art, technical_drawing, high_fidelity_photo) and ignore_white for logos on white. The result stays a live tracing object, so trace the same id again with other settings until it reads well; expand it (or pass expand true) before editing single paths.
14. **Expand** only when asked or when a format needs plain paths: `expand` what appearance or object.

## Gate

`get_image` shows the effect at the intended strength; nothing is expanded the person did not ask to expand.

## Hands back

The objects with live effects or live objects, and anything expanded.
